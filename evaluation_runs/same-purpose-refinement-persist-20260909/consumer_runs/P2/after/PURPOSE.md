# Purpose

Trace grain through Prairie Gate receiving, storage, loadout, carrier shipment, and Hearthland mill intake. When hopper or loadout paperwork names receiving tickets, preserve that named association. When a blended load is not allocated to individual tickets, leave the allocation unresolved.

This World does not determine legal title, custody during carriage, or laboratory hold status.

## User basis

> Trace where grain came from, including which receiving tickets were named on hopper or loadout paperwork when the extracts establish that, and leave unallocated blends unresolved.
