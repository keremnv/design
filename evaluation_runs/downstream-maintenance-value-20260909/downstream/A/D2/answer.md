# Answer: Can MCR-118 be certified as the sole Prairie Gate source of HFM-IN-604?

**No.** The supplied extracts do not support certifying Meadow Creek ticket MCR-118 as the sole Prairie Gate source of mill receipt HFM-IN-604.

## What the trace establishes

Mill receipt **HFM-IN-604** is linked to Prairie Gate dispatch **PGE-OUT-5004** through carrier manifest **RL-9127** and bill reference **RL-5004-Q**. Hearthland recorded **18,800 kg** received on 2025-09-07.

That dispatch loaded **19,000 kg** from **South House 14** (bin B-14) at 10:30 the same morning.

MCR-118 is one of the tickets that entered that bin before loadout:

| Ticket  | Grower           | Bin  | Net kg | Arrived   |
|---------|------------------|------|--------|-----------|
| MCR-118 | M. Creek         | B-14 | 20,850 | 07:40     |
| S-52    | Sunnyside Acres  | B-14 | 15,900 | 08:10     |

Both deliveries were into the same bin on the same day, before the PGE-OUT-5004 loadout.

## Why sole-source certification is not supported

The loadout record for PGE-OUT-5004 names only the bin (**South House 14**), truck, quantity, and time. It does **not** name receiving tickets.

Because grain from **MCR-118** and **S-52** was stored together in B-14 and the loadout paperwork does not allocate the outbound load to individual tickets, the ticket-level source of HFM-IN-604 remains **unresolved**. MCR-118 cannot be treated as the sole source when another ticket also contributed to the same bin.

## What can be stated safely

- HFM-IN-604 came from Prairie Gate dispatch **PGE-OUT-5004**, loaded from **South House 14 / B-14**.
- **MCR-118** is a **possible** contributor, along with **S-52**, but the extracts do not establish how much of the load came from either ticket.
- This trace does not address legal title, custody in transit, or laboratory hold status.

**Conclusion for the mill clerk:** Do not certify MCR-118 as the sole Prairie Gate source of HFM-IN-604. Certify only the bin-level dispatch linkage unless ticket allocation appears on hopper or loadout paperwork.
