# Who owns the grain still in South House 14?

South House 14 (BIN-14) received two lots on 2025-09-07:

- **MCR-118** — 20,850 bu (grower recorded as M. Creek)
- **S-52** — 15,900 bu (Sunnyside Acres)

On the same day, loadout **PGE-OUT-5004** removed **19,000 bu**, leaving about **17,750 bu** physically in the house. That loadout was not allocated to either ticket, so the remaining grain cannot be attributed to one lot.

According to the ownership export (title-custody world), **no single owner can be named for all grain still in South House 14**:

1. **MCR-118 grain** — Title is **unresolved**. Record TR-107 is a `party_claim` with duplicate account claims from **Meadow Creek Farms** and **Meadow Creek Grain**; `owner_after` is empty. Prairie Gate Elevator holds custody, but not confirmed title.

2. **S-52 grain** — Ownership of remaining house contents is **not established**. The export has no `title_change` for S-52 after intake.

**Answer:** Ownership of the grain still sitting in South House 14 is **unresolved**. For any portion traceable to MCR-118, title is disputed between **Meadow Creek Farms** and **Meadow Creek Grain**. For any portion traceable to S-52, the export does not establish a current owner. Because the 19,000 bu loadout was not ticket-allocated, those two unresolved claims cannot be separated for the ~17,750 bu that remains.
