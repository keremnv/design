# Purpose

Trace physical grain movement through Prairie Gate receiving, storage, loadout, carrier shipment, and Hearthland mill intake when the extracts establish those links. Preserve unsupported blend allocation, missing mill receipts, and unconfirmed cargo notes as unresolved.

This World does not determine current legal title, custody during carriage, or lab-hold status.

## User basis

> Trace where grain came from, what happened to it in the elevator, and where it went when the available evidence establishes those links.
