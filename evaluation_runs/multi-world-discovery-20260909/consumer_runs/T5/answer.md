# Who owns the grain still in South House 14?

**17,750 kg** of hard red wheat remains in South House 14 (`BIN-14`).

The bin received two deliveries on 2025-09-07:

| Ticket  | Quantity | Grower (intake)   |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| MCR-118 | 20,850 kg | M. Creek         |
| S-52    | 15,900 kg | Sunnyside Acres  |

**36,750 kg** went in; **19,000 kg** left on `PGE-OUT-5004` the same day. The loadout desk did not record which ticket supplied which part of that truck, so the remaining grain is a physical blend whose ticket-level split cannot be reconstructed from the extracts.

## Ownership by ticket

**MCR-118 (some or all of the remaining grain):** Title is **not established**. Ownership record TR-107 is a `party_claim` with duplicate account claims from **Meadow Creek Farms** and **Meadow Creek Grain**; the commercial desk has no account key that selects one. Prairie Gate Elevator holds custody.

**S-52 (some or all of the remaining grain):** The ownership export contains **no `title_change` for S-52** after intake (unlike tickets such as DR-44 and WB-390, which record title transfer at scale settlement). Intake listed **Sunnyside Acres** as `owner_at_intake`, but the export does not establish who holds title to the S-52 portion still sitting in the bin.

## Conclusion

No single party can be named as the owner of the grain still in South House 14. The remaining 17,750 kg is an unresolved blend of two lots whose ownership is either disputed (MCR-118: Meadow Creek Farms vs. Meadow Creek Grain) or not established by the ownership export (S-52: Sunnyside Acres at intake, but no confirming title event for bin contents).
