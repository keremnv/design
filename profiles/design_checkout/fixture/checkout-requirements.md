# Mobile checkout requirements

The mobile checkout must preserve purchase confidence. At the commitment
point, the shopper must be able to see the order total and the promo-code
entry. While entering payment details, the critical order summary (including
the total) must remain available without losing the payment task.

The requirements do not prescribe which of the total or promo code should
dominate the visual hierarchy. That is a design determination for the
constructor to make.
