# Ledger notes

The ledger is a hidden adjudication device. It is intentionally more explicit than any host-visible file. In particular:

- `M-202` is a blended outbound load with two established contributors, not one inbound load renamed at dispatch.
- `M-204` is linked to the South House 14 shipment and its processor receipt, but its individual contribution from `M-104` versus `M-105` is not established.
- `M-203` has a departure record and intended East mill route, but no processor receipt in the supplied evidence and no reliable source-ticket allocation.
- The Meadow Creek source party is unresolved between two named accounts. This is separate from the fact that the truck carrying `M-204` is known.
- A source owner is not interchangeable with the title holder at a later time, and a custodian is not interchangeable with either.
- A negative answer is permitted only when the relevant complete extract is explicitly in scope. Otherwise the correct consequence is `not established`, not `none exists`.
