# External provenance

## Published problem basis

The scenario is based on the published NIST grain-elevator-to-processor traceability use case by Farhad Ameri, Evan K. Wallace, Reid Yoder, and Frank Riddick (2023). The publication describes grain moving through receiving, storage, loadout, transport, processor receipt, sampling/observation, transformation, custody, and ownership contexts, and it reports competency-question themes used to validate the semantic model.

The baseline transfer/location question themes are adapted from the earlier NIST/ASME work by Ameri, Wallace, Yoder, and Riddick (2020). The hidden question files record the publication and source location for each adapted question.

## Reference/comparator materials

GS1 EPCIS and CBV and IOF/SCRO are comparator materials for the separate human-authored reference model only. The constructor is not exposed to their vocabularies, documents, URLs, or mappings.

No external paper, specification, dataset, RDF graph, ontology file, or proprietary operational record is redistributed in this benchmark. Only citations and URLs are retained. The operational evidence instance, identifiers, organizations, timestamps, quantities, notes, and hidden truth ledger are newly generated for this experiment.

The NIST publications are cited as the standard-backed problem basis; this benchmark does not claim to reproduce their original simulated dataset or knowledge graph.
