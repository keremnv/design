# Yard and desk notes

These notes accompany the daily exports from Prairie Gate Elevator and the receiving extracts from Hearthland Feed Mill.

## Local names

- `PG-ELEV` and `Prairie Gate` refer to the elevator at 17 County Route 8.
- The bin system calls `BIN-12` “North House 12” and `BIN-14` “South House 14”.
- The elevator desk uses `PGE-OUT-####`; the carrier bill and the mill receiving desk use their own numbers for the same truck movement.
- `HFM-N` is the North mill and `HFM-E` is the East mill. A carrier consignee code is not a mill receiving slip.

## How the exports are maintained

- Scale tickets are created at the receiving lane. The bin crew normally copies the ticket into `material_note`, but a single hopper move may combine several tickets.
- A loadout record identifies the bin and truck at the spout. It does not allocate a blended truck load back to individual receiving tickets unless the desk comment says so.
- The carrier manifest is the departure record. The mill creates a new local receiving slip when a truck is accepted at a dock.
- The receiving log supplied for HFM-North is complete for 2025-09-05 through 2025-09-07. No conclusion about other HFM sites follows from its absence.
- The scale and bin exports supplied here cover the listed tickets and movements for 2025-09-02 through 2025-09-07; the ownership export contains recorded changes, not a guarantee that an unrecorded agreement did not exist.

## Exceptions written on the packets

- The 12,000 kg truck on `PGE-OUT-5003` left with a carrier manifest, but no HFM receiving slip is in the supplied extracts.
- The route packet for that truck has a handwritten note `N3 / EF-18?`. `N3` is used as a route code elsewhere. The note is not a validated source ticket.
- Intake uses “M. Creek” for the `MCR-118` delivery. The commercial desk has claims from both Meadow Creek Farms and Meadow Creek Grain and has no account key that selects one.
- `PGE-OUT-5004` left from South House 14 after two deliveries were placed there. The loadout desk did not record which delivery supplied which part of that truck.
